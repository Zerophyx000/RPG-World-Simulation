### Lore
The Crystal caverns are the entry into the labyrinth. It has many secrets along it's walls and crevices. The Floor is made up of a single area, which is split into different zones. In the center of the floor, is the grand monument. Notable mysterious names are inscribed here onto a monolyth in the center and the area is one of the safest in the labyrinth. Here the entire area is nicely lit up and no monsters spawn. Then there is the regular crystal caverns. It's a dark area with some crystals providing lighting. Primarily there are 2 types of monsters spawning here, goblins and wolves. In this part they spawn exclusively alone and aren't really threatening. Then there is the dark zone, which is a dark empty zone with neither light nor walls. From here on it's necessary to have a navigator to locate one of the portals leading to the second floor.
