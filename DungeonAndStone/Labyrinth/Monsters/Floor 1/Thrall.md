### Lore
Thralls are former humans turned into abominations, after a vampire sucked their blood dry and forced them to become their subordinates. If close in proximity to a vampire, their stats will be boosted by 20% and they will substitute their life force if the vampire needs it. They commonly appear in floor 3 of the labyrinth, but will also make appearance in the bloody citadel rift of floor 1.
### Behaviour
Thralls behave similarly like ghouls, as they simply mindlessly attack any traveler they see. However, once a vampire appears nearby, it changes their behavior. A thrall will stop attacking travelers and instead defend the vampire, who would use ranged attacks behind their defense.
