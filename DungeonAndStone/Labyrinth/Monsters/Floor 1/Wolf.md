### Lore
Wolves are a tier 9 enemy that appears on both the light and dark zones of the first floor of the labyrinth.
They like to hunt and single out lone travelers, especially in the light zones, where they will even try to avoid full parties. Generally, they're not considered much of a threat both individually and in packs. 
### Behaviour
In the light zones of the first floor, wolves try to avoid parties of travelers and only attack single or weakened adventurers, basically only easy prey. They spawn alone. In the dark zones of the first floor, wolves appear in packs and try to outman even full parties. The biggest threat of a wolf is their bite attack, as it has a small chance to cause bleeding.
