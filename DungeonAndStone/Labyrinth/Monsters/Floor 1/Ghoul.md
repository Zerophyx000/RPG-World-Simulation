### Lore
The Ghoul is a weak enemy that appears on both the second floor of the labyrinth and in the first floor rift, the bloody citadel. It's a mindless monster that indiscriminately attacks whatever is in front of it. It has no special traits, except that it is immune to curses. That comes with the cost of being vulnerable to all elemental damage, and extremely vulnerable to holy damage. 
### Behaviour
No special behaviour, attacks whatever it sees. It's attacks are simply blunt hits, or damage with their weapon, if they carry one.
