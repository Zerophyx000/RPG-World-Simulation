### Lore
Goblins are considered the weakest enemy in the labyrinth. They hang around both the light and dark zones of the first floor. They are often in smaller packs, because the individual strength of a goblin isn't strong enough to even threaten a newbie traveler.
Goblins are also considered a semi-smart monster, as they actively use tools and traps to capture a traveler, despite their own weakness.
### Behaviour
Goblins walk around together in small packs in both the light and dark zones of the first floor. They utilize the help of tools and traps to capture newbie travelers that are either unprepared, alone, or both. The goblins in the light zones are usually considered easier to beat, as they are not much of a threat, since they do not have tools or traps. In the dark zones of the goblin area, they are considered a primary threat to newbie travelers, as they start using wooden clubs to up their damage and start acting a bit more scheming. They deploy traps to slow a traveler and inflict light bleeding onto their feet, to slow down a single party member and make it hard to flee, before they encircle them and attack.
