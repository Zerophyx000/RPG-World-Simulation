When someone dies, their eyes are closed and they are covered with a cloth. The family or friends wash the body and lay it out at home or in the chapel. Someone keeps vigil through the first night, so the dead are not alone; the watch is kept in turns of a few hours, and the one who is relieved goes to rest. The dead are buried in the chapel yard on the second or third day after the death. The oldest or most respected person present speaks some words; afterwards the mourners share bread. Taking from the house of the dead before the burial is shameful.
