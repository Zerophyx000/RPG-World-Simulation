**Right now.** A merchant caravan rolled into Brackenford at dawn and moved on, leaving four travellers behind on the village square. They have come to settle here: to find work, a roof and a fresh start. None of them has been here before, and they don't know the village: not where to eat, not where to sleep, not who is who. They walked through the night and are tired and hungry. Only the locals know their way around.
